HACKSAW WEBSITE REFRESH — 2026-07-24

Core policy changes
- Perk-bearing payments are described as digital support purchases, not donations.
- Amethyst is consistently £20+.
- All rank thresholds are cumulative: £20, £50, £100, £150, £250, £500, £1,000, £2,000.
- Every £1 awards 100 cosmetic HS Tokens.
- Every individual purchase of £20+ adds 30 days of Priority Queue.
- Refunded/reversed payments reduce the net Lifetime Support Total and related benefits may be adjusted.
- Bohemia approval is clearly separated from ownership/operation.

Evidence-hardening features
- Versioned Support Terms (2026-07-24).
- Dedicated Privacy Notice.
- Calculator for tokens, cumulative total, rank and newly crossed thresholds.
- Pre-payment confirmation generator for Discord tickets.
- Confirmation includes an acknowledgement ID, UTC timestamp, Discord ID, Steam ID64, payment method, amount, current/new cumulative total, expected rank, tokens and Priority Queue.
- Clear warning that browser acknowledgement is not stored until pasted into Discord.

Technical refresh
- Fully responsive modern dark visual design.
- No runtime dependency on Bootstrap, jQuery, Google Fonts or the old template scripts.
- Mobile navigation and accessible focus/skip-link support.
- Vercel security headers, robots.txt and sitemap.xml.
