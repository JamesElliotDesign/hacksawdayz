Tickets create a clearer support record

Use the ticket system for account linking, payment confirmation, technical problems and evidence. Keeping relevant information in one ticket helps staff resolve issues consistently.

  • Include your Discord user ID and Steam ID64.
  • Describe the issue and expected outcome clearly.
  • Upload unedited screenshots or clips where requested.
  • Do not publish private payment details in public channels.
