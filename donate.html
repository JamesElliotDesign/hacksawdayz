Independent-server disclosure

Hacksaw is an independently operated DayZ community server. Hacksaw is approved under Bohemia Interactive's DayZ server monetisation programme, but it is not owned, operated or managed by Bohemia Interactive and is not an official Bohemia Interactive server or service. A purchase supports Hacksaw, not Bohemia Interactive.

How the programme works

Every purchase has immediate benefits and cumulative progress

Your Lifetime Support Total is the net value of completed support purchases credited to your verified player account, after refunds and reversals.

01

Cosmetic tokens

Each £1 purchased awards 100 HS Tokens for eligible aesthetic in-game items.

02

Priority queue

Each individual purchase of £20 or more adds one month of priority queue access.

03

Cumulative ranks

Net purchases stack toward rank thresholds. Each threshold unlocks its corresponding rank card and listed benefits.

04

Reversals adjust totals

Refunded or reversed amounts are deducted from the Lifetime Support Total and related benefits may be adjusted.

Meaning of “for life” and “unlimited”

“Priority Queue for life” means for the lifetime of the eligible player account while Hacksaw continues to operate and offer that feature. “Unlimited HS Tokens” remains cosmetic-only, account-bound and subject to fair-use, technical and availability limits. Neither benefit has cash value.
