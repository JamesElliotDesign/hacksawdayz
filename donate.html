How contributions are calculated

Simple immediate benefits, cumulative rank progress

  • Each £1 adds 100 cosmetic HS Tokens.
  • An individual contribution of £20 or more adds 30 days of Priority Queue.
  • Every completed contribution adds to your net Lifetime Support Total.
  • Crossing a rank threshold credits the corresponding Rank ID Card.
  • Discord Server Boost credit is non-stackable and counts as £3.50 towards rank progress only.

Handled automatically in Discord

A short confirmation, a complete record

The support bot now records the verified reference, account links, terms version, benefits, fulfilment and later reward claims in the background.

  1. 01

    Choose a method

    Follow the instructions in the relevant Discord channel and keep the reference or transaction ID supplied by the payment service.

  2. 02

    Open a private ticket

    Provide the payment reference or transaction ID and your Steam ID64 if it is not already linked.

  3. 03

    Review and confirm

    The bot shows the amount, tokens, Priority Queue result, rank progress and any newly unlocked Rank ID Cards. Check the details and press Confirm.

Meaning of “lifetime” and “unlimited”

Lifetime Priority Queue applies for the lifetime of the eligible player account while Hacksaw continues to operate and offer the feature. Unlimited HS Tokens remain cosmetic-only, account-bound and subject to fair-use, technical and availability limits.
