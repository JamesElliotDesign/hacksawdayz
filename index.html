Transparency first

Approved to monetise. Still independently operated.

Hacksaw appears on Bohemia Interactive's approved DayZ server monetisers list. That approval permits compliant server monetisation; it does not mean Hacksaw is owned, operated or managed by Bohemia Interactive.

  • No weapons, vehicles, currency or gameplay advantages are sold.
  • HS Tokens are restricted to eligible cosmetic crafting.
  • Support purchases and cumulative rank benefits are described before payment.
