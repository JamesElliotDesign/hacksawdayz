Rules can be clarified as server systems and events change. Where a Discord announcement conflicts with this summary, contact staff before acting.

01

General rules

  • Owners and administrators may remove players or teams whose conduct materially harms the server or community.
  • Permanent-ban offences include duping, hacking, cheating, racism, sexism, homophobia, hate speech, serious toxicity and impersonating staff.
  • English is required in global text and voice channels.
  • Do not camp or log out at points of interest.
  • All points of interest must be claimed in accordance with the Discord POI-claiming rules.
  • POI turret systems can ruin vehicles, including helicopters, within marked red no-vehicle zones.
  • Do not repeatedly loot-cycle the same zone within a restart period.
  • Crafted items may not be bought, sold or gifted between players unless a current event rule expressly allows it.
  • HS Tokens may not be gifted, transferred or spent on behalf of another player.
  • Report suspected cheating privately through the ticket system; do not call players out publicly.
  • Staff may request clips or other evidence for any ticket.
  • Lost items are not replaced without evidence meeting the compensation policy.
  • Do not stream other servers or games in Hacksaw voice channels.
  • Public voice channels are for Hacksaw players.
  • Do not loot player bodies in PvE.
02

Base rules

  • One headquarters or base is permitted per player or team.
  • Maximum 10 foundation/floor pieces, three floors and 10 floor kits per floor.
  • You may build inside structures that are not within a military zone or point of interest.
  • Maximum 10 base-storage containers, 10 functional base items and one crafting bench per player. Trash cans do not count.
  • Boosting or raiding decaying bases is not allowed.
  • Secure bases and storage with a codelock.
  • Staff conduct base checks. Non-compliant bases may be resized or relocated.
  • Hunter camps are no-build zones.
03

Vehicle rules

  • Do not damage player vehicles.
  • Do not loot or steal unlocked vehicles.
  • Store vehicles in personal or public garages.
  • Vehicles left on the map for more than 24 hours may be repossessed.
  • Only one active vehicle per player may be on the map at a time.
  • A licence is required to own, buy or use helicopters and exotic vehicles.
  • If two or more group members hold a helicopter licence, the group may buy a helipad and share one helicopter. Helicopters left outside a helipad may be repossessed.
  • Do not drive or fly into red-circle zones.
04

Safe-zone rules

  • No stealing, scamming or griefing items or vehicles.
  • No voice-chat abuse or deliberate trolling.
  • Abandoned vehicles may be towed after one hour.
05

Raiding rules

  • Raiding is limited to verified POIs through authorised events.
  • Raiding groups may contain up to four members, and at least one member must hold the required licence.
  • Player bases may not be raided or boosted into.
  • Bases may not be given away. Notify an administrator if you are leaving.
06

Gifting rules

  • Permitted items may be gifted in person only.
  • Crafted items and HS Tokens may not be gifted or transferred.
07

Compensation

Vehicle claims normally considered

  • Vehicle stolen from a safe zone, unless repossessed.
  • Vehicle destroyed illegally.
  • Bugged or inaccessible vehicle inventory.
  • Vehicle lost to a confirmed cheater.
  • Vehicle lost on restart or server crash.

Vehicle claims normally denied

  • Destroyed by AI or owner error.
  • Lost lock code.
  • Destroyed by a no-fly/no-vehicle-zone turret.
  • Repossession, unlocked theft or water damage.

Item claims normally considered

  • Items stolen from a safe zone.
  • Purchased trader items not delivered.
  • Loss caused by a confirmed cheater, restart or crash.
  • Crafted or supporter cosmetic items where both crafting evidence and a server issue are shown.

Item claims normally denied

  • Expired tombstones, ruined items or ordinary loss.
  • Items stolen by AI or from unlocked storage.
  • Items lost through PvP, turret damage or hover-loot behaviour.
08

HS Tokens

  • HS Tokens are server-bound virtual cosmetic credits with no cash value.
  • Tokens may not be sold, gifted, transferred or used for another player.
  • Token delivery and redemption are recorded against the verified account.
  • Tokens lost through normal player action may not be replaced; confirmed server faults are reviewed under the compensation policy.
  • Stealing or exploiting HS Tokens may result in removal from the server.
