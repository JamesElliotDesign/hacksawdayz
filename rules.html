Core principle

Do not damage the server experience for other players.

Owners and administrators may remove individuals or groups whose behaviour has a sustained negative impact, even where a situation is not covered word-for-word below.

01

General conduct

Behaviour, chat and fair play across the community.
  • Duping, hacking, cheating, exploit abuse, staff impersonation and association with known cheating activity may result in a permanent ban.
  • Racism, sexism, homophobia, hate speech, bigotry, severe toxicity and targeted harassment are not tolerated.
  • English is required in global text and voice chat so moderation remains possible.
  • Do not ping staff for routine support. Use the correct ticket or report system.
  • Do not call suspected cheaters out publicly. Submit evidence privately.
  • Clips may be requested for moderation, compensation or technical claims.
  • Public voice channels are for Hacksaw players. Do not stream other servers or unrelated games there.
  • Defamatory campaigns, repeated staff-bashing and behaviour intended to undermine the community may result in removal.
02

PvE and player damage

Player damage is enabled, but PvP is not the game mode.

Do

  • Treat unexpected player damage as an incident to report, not an invitation to retaliate.
  • Use a ticket if you believe you were attacked or if AI appeared outside its expected zone.
  • Manage team crossfire carefully; friendly fire remains possible.

Do not

  • Shoot, kill, bait, grief or retaliate against other players.
  • Loot player bodies or tombstones in PvE.
  • Turn disputes over vehicles, bases, POIs or items into combat.

AI normally spawn within marked red-circle zones and dynamic-event areas. Report suspicious behaviour rather than escalating it.

03

POIs, claims and dynamic events

Claim before engagement, respect cooldowns and keep vehicles out.
  • Every listed POI must be claimed. You must be within 500 metres to claim it.
  • Nearby teammates within 100 metres are added to the claim group automatically.
  • T1–T4 claims last 45 minutes; T5 claims last 60 minutes.
  • After a claim ends, the POI enters a 45-minute cooldown.
  • A player or group cannot reclaim the same POI until the next three-hour restart cycle.
  • Grace may be activated once for the last claimed POI after it enters cooldown. It lasts 15 minutes and applies to the eligible claim group.
  • Only cancel a claim before the POI has been started. Repeated command misuse may lead to a warning.
  • Do not camp, log out, loot-cycle, intentionally spawn AI or cheese POIs.
  • POIs are no-vehicle zones. Turrets can destroy cars and helicopters inside the red markers.
  • Dynamic events claim automatically when a player enters the event zone; they do not require a manual claim.

04

Bases, upkeep and security

Your HQ, nails and codelocks are your responsibility.
  • A Territory HQ must be placed before other base items. Items outside its protected range may be deleted.
  • Each player must authorise themselves and rescan territory after placing new items.
  • All bases require nails in the HQ upkeep slot. No nails means decay.
  • Secure doors, gates, containers and benches with a correctly coded codelock. Merely attaching a lock without setting the code is not enough.
  • No raiding, boosting, damaging or looting player bases. Do not loot unsecured bases.
  • Players cannot give away bases. Inform staff if you are leaving.
  • Base checks take place. Bases judged to create an unfair advantage may be resized, relocated or removed.
  • Decayed, open or abandoned bases may be removed. Valuable exposed storage may be moved to CAR for auction.
  • Staff do not replace structures, loot or access lost through failed upkeep or incorrectly secured locks.
No nails→No upkeep→Decay
05

Vehicles, storage and Capital Auto Recovery

Use garages. Leaving a vehicle out means accepting repo risk.
  • Do not damage, steal, loot or grief player vehicles. Unlocked vehicles are still not free to take on Chernarus.
  • Store vehicles and helicopters in personal, group or public virtual garages.
  • Only one active personal vehicle may be left on the map at a time.
  • Helicopters and exotic vehicles require the appropriate licence.
  • Vehicles left out for more than 12 hours may be recovered by Capital Auto Recovery.
  • A six-hour warning and a twelve-hour eligibility notice may be posted before recovery.
  • Moving a vehicle slightly does not reset responsibility. Do not hide vehicles inside bases to avoid repo.
  • Once repo'd, a vehicle is not privately returned, refunded or handed back. It enters the Sunday auction process.
  • Use the garage dump function to dispose of a vehicle; do not use repo as a disposal service.
  • Do not drive or fly into red-circle restricted zones.
CAR

Auctions are part of the system

Players may bid on any repo'd vehicle at the Sunday auction, including a vehicle they previously owned.

06

Safe zones, gifting and account-bound items

Respect protected spaces and keep restricted items with their owner.
  • No stealing, scamming, griefing or voice-chat trolling in safe zones.
  • Vehicles abandoned in safe zones may be towed after one hour.
  • Players may gift ordinary items in person only.
  • Crafted items cannot be bought, sold, gifted or loaned to other players.
  • Players cannot use crafted items they did not craft.
  • HS Tokens, Reward Tokens and other token types cannot be traded, gifted or spent for another player.
  • Supporter benefits and claimable rewards are account-bound.
07

Compensation and evidence

Server faults can be reviewed; ordinary loss and player mistakes are not insured.

Claims that may be approved

  • Vehicle destroyed illegally or stolen from a safe zone.
  • Vehicle or item loss caused by a verified server restart, crash or cheater.
  • Bugged or inaccessible vehicle inventory.
  • Items not delivered by a trader.
  • Player body looted in PvE.
  • Crafted/support items lost to a verified server issue with suitable crafting and incident evidence.

Claims normally denied

  • AI damage, owner mistakes, water damage or turret zones.
  • Repo action, lost lock codes or unlocked storage.
  • Ruined, dropped, misplaced or ordinarily lost items.
  • Despawned tombstones after the recovery window.
  • Items lost in PvP, hover loot or unsecured storage.
  • Claims without the evidence staff reasonably request.
T6

Takistan expedition rules

End-game PvE with limited support and no vehicle compensation.
Takistan is not for the light-hearted.

Prepare high-calibre weapons, custom ammunition, strong armour, HS gear, food, drink and medication before crossing over.

  • There are no safe zones. PvP is still prohibited everywhere.
  • Staff support and compensation are extremely limited.
  • AI roam beyond marked areas. Expect T6 units, snipers, grenadiers, commanders, paratroopers and air support.
  • No looting player bodies or tombstones.
  • Storage is limited to what fits within your expedition tent HQ. Secure it with codelocks.
  • Each player may place one personal garage meter with one slot; group garages have one shared slot.
  • Vehicles outside a personal or group garage are generally free to take unless currently in use or beside a currently claimed POI.
  • Do not damage, threaten or attack players over vehicles or property.
  • Forbidden City is the only no-drive/no-fly zone. Do not camp, log or abuse the area.
  • Vehicle compensation is not available. Item compensation is limited to crafted items lost through a verified server issue with video evidence.
08

Staff support, tickets and patience

Use tracked channels and treat volunteers with respect.
  • General chat is not a guaranteed support channel. Open the correct ticket so your issue can be tracked.
  • A delayed answer does not mean you are being ignored. Staff may be handling reports, development, tools or real-life commitments.
  • Provide complete information, including Steam ID64, relevant order/reference numbers and unedited evidence.
  • Repeated complaints, passive-aggressive remarks, entitlement or abuse towards staff may lead to warnings, mutes or removal.
  • Hacksaw may change systems, mods, balance or content when required. Compensation is not provided merely because a system changes.
  • The server restarts every three hours: 00:00, 03:00, 06:00, 09:00, 12:00, 15:00, 18:00 and 21:00 GMT.
